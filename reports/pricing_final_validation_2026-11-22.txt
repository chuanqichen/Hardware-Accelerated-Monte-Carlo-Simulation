Multi-lane
  This is the first end-to-end simulation of two and four real lanes with
  the round_sat version of price_dp and the registered accumulator tree.
  Sum, count and status match the Python model in all 24 array runs,
  including N not a multiple of the lane count.

One result per clock (initiation interval 1)
  tb_mc_top checks sustained II=1 through the whole one-lane pipeline and
  fails if it is violated. The multi-lane accumulator vectors include a
  2,000-cycle stretch of back-to-back full beats at 2 and 4 lanes.
  The standalone tb_price_dp and tb_exp2_unit insert a random idle cycle
  about one cycle in eight, so their longest back-to-back runs are 46 and
  91 cycles.

Throughput (results/pricing_throughput.csv)
  Pre-layout, standalone block synthesis, gscl45nm typical. price_dp
  (EXP_ADDR_BITS=10, MUL_STAGES=1) and the accumulator at 1, 2 and 4 lanes
  all meet setup timing at 5.00, 4.75 and 4.00 ns in Design Compiler. At the
  5 ns target that would be 2.0e8, 4.0e8 and 8.0e8 trials/s for 1, 2 and 4
  lanes. These are not routed numbers and not a synthesized multi-lane top;
  the team's PrimeTime run on the one-lane top reports negative setup slack,
  so 200 MHz is a target, not an achieved frequency.

Not covered
  Synthesis of a 2- or 4-lane top (the synthesis gate still allows one lane).
  Gate-level simulation and post-route timing of these changes.
  Hold and max-capacitance violations reported by synthesis.
